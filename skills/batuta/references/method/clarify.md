# Clarify — before routing an ambiguous request

Read when Step 1 finds the request ambiguous, creative or larger than one
sitting. The outcome is inline planning in the conversation, or a handoff
to `/batuta-plan`. No design document.

## Procedure

1. **Understand first.** Read the profile's Project map and whatever the request names. Ask nothing you can find out yourself.
2. **One question at a time.** Through the runtime's question tool; without one, make the question your whole message and stop. Closed decision → two to four mutually exclusive options, the recommended one first with its impact in one clause; free text accepted. Wait for the answer before the next question. Two or three questions is the norm; more means the request wants `/batuta-plan`.
3. **Two or three approaches**, each with its trade-off in one line, when the shape of the solution is open. Recommend one. Never present one approach as if it were the only one.
4. **Sketch the plan in prose**: the tasks (Step 1.5's unit), their order, the lane you predict for each. Plain text in the conversation.
5. **Preserve executable requirements literally.** Versions, paths, commands, flags, whitespace and constraints the user typed enter the brief byte for byte. Never normalize, upgrade or paraphrase them.

*Done when:* the user confirmed the sketch, or asked for `/batuta-plan`.

## Never

- Guess a default the user did not give.
- Ask what the codebase already answers.
- Turn approval of the sketch into another round of questions.
