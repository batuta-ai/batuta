## batuta-write

Derived from Siqi Chen's [humanizer skill](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), metadata version `3.0.0`, at commit [`9862685f575c65a8247f90369951df1b3416e3d6`](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/tree/9862685f575c65a8247f90369951df1b3416e3d6).

The adaptation retains evidence-preserving edits, intentional voice and prose-as-data boundaries. It omits the long pattern catalog, word and punctuation bans, invented reactions, authorship judgments and default draft-plus-final output. It adds explicit preservation of machine-readable planning contracts and remains usable without a Batuta plan.
